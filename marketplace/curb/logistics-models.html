Logistics Models

Two complementary modes, not competing — the choice depends on the object's weight/size and geographic scope, not a preference of principle.
Peer courier / crowdshipping
The only viable mode for bulky local objects (a sofa) and for the cross-border corridor. Mechanism: algorithmic dispatch, bid/price proposal by the carrier, reuse of trips already planned (personal vehicle, a flight with free baggage allowance, a regular shuttle).
Parcel carriers — Hermès, Mondial Relay
For light parcels (≤10kg), national, no availability constraint. Rates judged very competitive, native parcel tracking, immediate drop-off with no intermediation step — a logic close to Vinted.

Explicit and deliberate exclusion: DHL, FedEx. A positioning choice, not an oversight — judged expensive and unreliable by comparison.

Distinctive advantage to note: tracking. Native with parcel carriers, absent on the crowdshipping side unless the user adds their own device (e.g. AirTag).
